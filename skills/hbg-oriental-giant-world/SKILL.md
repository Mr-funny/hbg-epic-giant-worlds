# HBG Oriental Giant World

Transform subject matter into the HBG Eastern Colossal Style Lock without copying a reference composition or erasing the source culture.

## Read first

- Read `references/oriental-style-lock.md` completely.
- Read `references/prompt-contract.md` before writing the still and motion prompts.
- For image generation, use the environment's image-generation skill and obey visible-delivery rules.
- For video creation, read the environment's mandatory video skill first. Keep Gemini/Veo when explicitly requested.

## Accepted inputs

- one keyword: `道教`, `印度尼西亚`, `月球`, `弹琴`;
- a rough scene idea;
- an existing image prompt;
- a reference image or video;
- a prompt-bank question selected by a roaming skill.

If a reference is supplied, extract only transferable style signals: material precision, atmosphere, light, palette, lens energy, and motion rhythm. Do not inherit its circular gate, centered axis, central figure, symmetric courtyard, tree frame, or any other composition skeleton unless the user explicitly requests that composition.

## Transformation contract

Preserve:

- the source subject, action, cultural identity, symbolic meaning, and core wonder;
- culturally specific architecture, ritual boundaries, clothing, ecology, and materials;
- one memorable semantic hook from the source.

Transform:

- spatial design into a credible multi-layer colossal world;
- lighting into luminous, airy, auspicious, high-clarity cinema;
- scale into tiny-human-versus-megastructure contrast;
- composition into a new, non-template wide-angle arrangement;
- motion into slow, image-relative, rigid-anchor Veo language.

Do not “Chinese-wash” Thailand, Indonesia, Hinduism, Buddhism, or other living cultures. The target Eastern aesthetic is a spatial, material, tonal, and cinematic grammar; regional identity remains dominant. Use fully Chinese mythic architecture only when the subject supports it.

## Style lock

Unless the user explicitly asks for darkness or horror:

- luminous, airy, auspicious, serene, sacred, inviting;
- readable high-key image with blue-white atmosphere and warm dawn or dusk gold;
- ivory stone, pale jade, aged timber, vermilion, bronze, restrained gold, regional materials;
- realistic air perspective, volumetric clouds or mist, water vapor, and long sightlines;
- one giant form, one atmospheric or celestial anchor, tiny people, generous negative space;
- Chinese mythic creatures must be slender, elegant, auspicious, and culturally recognizable—not Western attacking dragons.

Avoid default storm-grey grading, crushing black foregrounds, horror faces, apocalyptic debris, neon sci-fi, generic European castles, Japanese shrine substitution, visual clutter, and character close-ups.

## Composition anti-template gate

Before each new design, name the previous foreground family, camera relationship, subject position, and leading-line geometry. Change at least three of the four.

Rotate among:

- monumental gate, eave, colonnade, wall opening;
- canyon, cave, cliff, volcanic shelf, ice wall;
- water plane, waterfall curtain, rain veil, boat bow;
- statue base, relief wall, ceremonial vessel, giant bridge;
- open ground with shadow or extreme proximity;
- vegetation only when semantically justified.

Support low, eye-level, high-oblique, aerial, and selective top-down views. A batch of four should feel like four discoveries in one world, not four skins on one layout.

## Still workflow

1. Produce an adaptation card: source semantics, preserved identity, new visual thesis, selected scale mechanism, camera, foreground, atmosphere, materials, light, and prohibitions.
2. Write the exact 16:9 still prompt using the Style Lock.
3. If requested, generate and inspect the mother image.
4. Reject reference-composition copying, malformed architecture, fake writing, duplicated people, generic fantasy substitution, over-darkness, and collapsed depth.
5. Deliver only after the image is visibly present in chat and saved to an absolute path.

## Motion workflow

Write from the actual approved image. Choose exactly one main camera path: stabilized dolly, crane rise, lateral arc, descending reveal, or slow axial passage. Add restrained environment motion already supported by the image: layered cloud drift, waterfall spray, fabric, incense, foliage, water reflection, dust, rain, or light breathing.

Architecture, terrain, deity form, planet, moon, bridge, and other giant anchors remain rigid. People perform at most one small plausible action. No new buildings, creatures, symbols, crowds, readable text, energy beams, weather replacement, scene cut, time-lapse, fast orbit, or camera collision.

## Output contract

Always return the adaptation card, complete still prompt, complete image-relative motion prompt, and explicit constraints. When generating media, also return the visible still, saved files, verified video specifications, and QA notes.
