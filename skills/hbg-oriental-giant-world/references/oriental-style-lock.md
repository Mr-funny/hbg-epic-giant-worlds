# HBG Eastern Colossal Style Lock v1.0

## Constant visual DNA

- Emotional target: wonder, serenity, sacred distance, invitation, and cognitive scale shock.
- Exposure: high-key and readable; shadows frame rather than swallow the image.
- Atmosphere: clear blue-white air, layered cloud or mist, realistic aerial perspective, soft volumetric light.
- Warm accent: dawn gold, peach gold, restrained temple gold, candle or incense warmth.
- Materials: ivory stone, pale jade, aged timber, vermilion lacquer, bronze, silk, ceramic, regional stone and metal.
- Scale: tiny humans, one dominant giant form, long spatial intervals, generous negative space.
- Detail: cinematic realism, coherent construction, precise ornament, natural wear, physically plausible light.
- Motion: slow stabilized camera, layered parallax, restrained environmental action, rigid architecture.

## Variable subject layer

The Style Lock does not erase identity.

- Chinese subjects may use jade terraces, timber halls, dougong, flying eaves, cloud scrolls, mountains-and-seas creatures, and celestial palaces.
- Thai subjects retain Thai roof profiles, stupas, naga language, tropical light, waterways, and local materials.
- Indonesian subjects retain volcanic geography, maritime space, candi silhouettes, carved stone, tropical vegetation, and regional ritual rhythm.
- Hindu subjects retain iconographic consistency, temple geometry, ghats, lamps, garlands, river or mountain cosmology; never randomly mix deity attributes.
- Buddhist subjects choose one regional tradition and do not merge Han, Tibetan, Theravada, and Japanese forms indiscriminately.

Eastern style here means spatial rhythm, material restraint, atmosphere, sacred scale, and contemplative human behavior—not replacing every culture with Chinese architecture.

## Giant contrast stack

Prefer this stack:

1. one close readable cultural object or material surface;
2. a tiny human stage;
3. one impossible but coherent giant structure or being;
4. vast atmosphere, terrain, or water;
5. optionally one familiar cosmic anchor.

Keep the layers spatially separated. The cosmic anchor gets negative space and cannot become generic space opera.

## Composition rotation

Foreground families: gate/eave/column; cliff/cave/rock; water/rain/waterfall; statue/relief/craft; open shadow/ground; vegetation only when justified.

Camera families: ground-skimming low; eye-level; oblique high; aerial with horizon; selective near-vertical top-down.

Leading lines: diagonal rise; axial road; lateral wall; S-curve river; spiral terrace; radial plan; layered horizontal bands; open negative-space reveal.

For consecutive outputs, change at least three of foreground, camera, subject position, and leading line.

## Reference firewall

A reference may supply:

- clarity and material quality;
- palette and light relationship;
- air, haze, cloud, water, and cinematic finish;
- broad movement tempo.

A reference does not automatically supply:

- its gate shape;
- central axis or symmetry;
- figure placement;
- tree framing;
- courtyard layout;
- exact lens or crop;
- object count or silhouette.

Write a new composition statement before adapting the style.

## Default prohibitions

No default gloom, horror, apocalypse, dead-black foreground mass, storm-grey wash, neon cyberpunk, spacecraft, hologram UI, multiple planets, European castle substitution, Japanese shrine substitution, dense crowd, character close-up, combat, malformed ornament, fake scripture, text, subtitle, logo, or watermark.

For Chinese mythic creatures: no bat wings, bulky Western-dragon anatomy, attack pose, monster face, or fire-breathing cliché unless explicitly requested.
