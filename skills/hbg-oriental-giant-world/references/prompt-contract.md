# Eastern colossal prompt contract

## Adaptation card

```text
Source subject and semantic hook:
Culture or tradition to preserve:
What must not be inherited from the reference:
Reality baseline to reject:
Eastern colossal thesis:
Giant transmutation and fused categories:
Impossible law and two visible consequences:
Three independent scale proofs:
Foreground family:
Human inhabitation and tiny action:
Atmospheric or cosmic anchor:
World-body or giant construct:
Camera, lens, horizon, leading line:
Palette, materials, natural light:
Cultural and continuity risks:
Absurdity score / 10:
```

## Still prompt requirements

Specify exact 16:9 landscape, a distinct wide-angle camera relationship, foreground-midground-background separation, tiny people inhabiting the giant form, one category-fused world-body controlling roughly half or more of the image, one impossible law with environmental consequences, at least three scale proofs, optional single celestial anchor, Style Lock palette and exposure, culturally correct architecture and behavior, and detailed negative constraints.

Quality labels are secondary. The prompt must describe an impossible premise, spatial engineering, cognitive contradiction, and realistic physical rendering. Reject plausible tourism scenery and merely enlarged landmarks.

## Veo/Gemini requirements

The final motion prompt is written after inspecting the approved still. It must:

- name real visible anchors and positions;
- keep the giant form and architecture rigid;
- choose one principal camera path;
- create parallax among foreground, stage, atmosphere, and distant structure;
- choose one environment-motion family and one tiny human action;
- preserve exact palette, light direction, lens feeling, and cultural identity;
- forbid additions, redesign, morphing, enlargement, false writing, cuts, fast motion, and style shift.

If the still is not available, provide a provisional motion plan and state that it must be rewritten from the actual image.
