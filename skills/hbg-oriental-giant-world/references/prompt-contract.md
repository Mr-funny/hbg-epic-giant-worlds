## Still prompt requirements

Specify exact 16:9 landscape, a distinct wide-angle camera relationship, foreground-midground-background separation, tiny people, one giant form, optional single celestial anchor, Style Lock palette and exposure, culturally correct architecture and behavior, and detailed negative constraints.

Quality labels are secondary. The prompt must describe spatial engineering and contrast.

## Veo/Gemini requirements

The final motion prompt is written after inspecting the approved still. It must:

- name real visible anchors and positions;
- keep the giant form and architecture rigid;
- choose one principal camera path;
- create parallax among foreground, stage, atmosphere, and distant structure;
- choose one environment-motion family and one tiny human action;
- preserve exact palette, light direction, lens feeling, and cultural identity;
- forbid additions, redesign, morphing, enlargement, false writing, cuts, fast motion, and style shift.

If the still is not available, provide a provisional motion plan and state that it must be rewritten from the actual image.
