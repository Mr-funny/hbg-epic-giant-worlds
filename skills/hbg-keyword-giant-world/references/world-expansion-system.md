# Keyword expansion and “everything becomes a giant construct” system

## Start with transmutation, not scenery

Do not expand a keyword into a list of plausible landmarks. First choose what the keyword itself becomes at world scale:

- terrain or anatomy;
- inhabited architecture or infrastructure;
- weather or hydrology;
- a creature functioning as environment;
- a craft or material functioning as geology;
- an action functioning as a physical law;
- a shadow, cavity, reflection, or absence functioning as a world.

Then add cultural environment, people, light, and camera around that transformed premise.

## Classify before visualizing

| Input type | Expand through | Avoid reducing it to |
|---|---|---|
| Country or region | transform one landscape, craft, daily object, animal, built form, or material into an impossible inhabited world | flag, army, tourist collage, normal landmark montage |
| Religion | transform sacred geometry, ritual space, symbolic object, light, pilgrimage, or cosmology into an impossible spatial law | invented deity portrait, false scripture, mixed sects, merely enlarged temple |
| Civilization or era | ecology, settlement, engineering, materials, social scale | anachronism, costume pile-up |
| City or landmark | terrain, street or water system, silhouette, lived rhythm | one postcard landmark |
| Myth or deity | symbolic landscape, ritual space, creature language, cosmic rule | game-effects clutter |
| Action or object | make the gesture, process, sound, cavity, surface, or silhouette become inhabitable terrain or physical law | isolated product close-up, merely oversized prop |
| Abstract concept | make one spatial rule or material metaphor visibly govern a civilization | generic particles and glow |

## Eight expansion slots

Select one or two strong elements per slot:

1. core spirit;
2. representative environment;
3. architecture or engineering language;
4. material and craft;
5. symbol or pattern;
6. quiet human action;
7. one impossible keyword transmutation controlling the world;
8. light, weather, and atmosphere.

Optionally add one cosmic scale anchor: readable cloud horizon, Earth limb, giant moon or sun, eclipse, planetary horizon, crater plain. It creates comparison; it must not replace the keyword.

## Five depth layers

1. Foreground device from architecture, terrain, water, sculpture, craft, weather, or vegetation.
2. Midground human stage: platform, road, bridge, bank, courtyard, vessel, stair, or terrace.
3. Tiny people performing one plausible action.
4. Atmospheric expanse: sea, desert, cloud, mountain, jungle, snow, mist, or sky.
5. One primary world-body or giant construct derived from the keyword and fused with at least one other category.

## Scale mechanisms

- wide-angle proximity;
- tiny-human comparison;
- repeated-but-nonduplicated architectural rhythm;
- long leading lines;
- atmospheric compression and aerial perspective;
- a familiar celestial or planetary body;
- engineering that crosses incompatible domains, such as a road through clouds or a temple carved into a tidal cliff;
- an ordinary cultural object transformed into inhabited landscape.
- anatomy, garment, creature, shadow, cavity, craft, or action transformed into an inhabited world system;
- a visible impossible law with environmental consequences.

Use at least three scale mechanisms together. Do not replace design with the words `8K`, `epic`, or `PBR`.

## Reality rejection

Fail the concept if it remains a plausible travel photograph after removing quality adjectives. `Temple + volcano`, `coastal gate + temple`, and `village + huge roof` are scenic combinations, not sufficient transmutations.

Require:

1. the keyword itself changes category;
2. one impossible law changes water, weather, light, gravity, horizon, or habitation;
3. people visibly use the construct;
4. three independent scale proofs remain readable.

Read `colossal-transmutation.md` and require at least 8/10 before prompting.

## Variation gate

For a batch, fill a grid before prompting:

| Shot | Camera | Foreground | Leading line | Giant form | Cosmic anchor | Motion |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | low | carved skin/base | diagonal pilgrimage route | body as terrain | sun | dolly + crane |
| 2 | eye | water/corridor | axial passage | object as inhabited infrastructure | none | lateral drift |
| 3 | high oblique | cliff/roof | spiral/terrace | creature as environment | cloud horizon | descending reveal |
| 4 | aerial/top-down | open/weather | radial/river | action or craft as physical law | Earth/moon | crane back/slow descent |

Change at least three columns between neighboring shots.

## Cultural checks

- Does the space remain identifiable after removing costumes?
- Are landscape, architecture, materials, and ritual mutually plausible?
- Is sacred writing absent or genuinely supplied by the user?
- Are living people treated as participants rather than exotic spectacle?
- Is one denomination or regional tradition chosen coherently?
- Does the cosmic anchor support rather than erase the culture?
