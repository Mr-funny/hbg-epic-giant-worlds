# Keyword expansion and colossal composition system

## Classify before visualizing

| Input type | Expand through | Avoid reducing it to |
|---|---|---|
| Country or region | landscape, built heritage, craft, daily gesture, spiritual atmosphere | flag, army, tourist collage |
| Religion | sacred space, geometry, light, pilgrimage, contemplation, community | invented deity portrait, false scripture, mixed sects |
| Civilization or era | ecology, settlement, engineering, materials, social scale | anachronism, costume pile-up |
| City or landmark | terrain, street or water system, silhouette, lived rhythm | one postcard landmark |
| Myth or deity | symbolic landscape, ritual space, creature language, cosmic rule | game-effects clutter |
| Action or object | physical gesture, material process, acoustic rhythm, spatial metaphor | isolated product close-up |
| Abstract concept | one spatial rule, one material metaphor, one human behavior | generic particles and glow |

## Eight expansion slots

Select one or two strong elements per slot:

1. core spirit;
2. representative environment;
3. architecture or engineering language;
4. material and craft;
5. symbol or pattern;
6. quiet human action;
7. one transformed megastructure;
8. light, weather, and atmosphere.

Optionally add one cosmic scale anchor: readable cloud horizon, Earth limb, giant moon or sun, eclipse, planetary horizon, crater plain. It creates comparison; it must not replace the keyword.

## Five depth layers

1. Foreground device from architecture, terrain, water, sculpture, craft, weather, or vegetation.
2. Midground human stage: platform, road, bridge, bank, courtyard, vessel, stair, or terrace.
3. Tiny people performing one plausible action.
4. Atmospheric expanse: sea, desert, cloud, mountain, jungle, snow, mist, or sky.
5. One primary megastructure derived from the keyword.

## Scale mechanisms

- wide-angle proximity;
- tiny-human comparison;
- repeated-but-nonduplicated architectural rhythm;
- long leading lines;
- atmospheric compression and aerial perspective;
- a familiar celestial or planetary body;
- engineering that crosses incompatible domains, such as a road through clouds or a temple carved into a tidal cliff;
- an ordinary cultural object transformed into inhabited landscape.

Use two or three mechanisms together. Do not replace design with the words `8K`, `epic`, or `PBR`.

## Variation gate

For a batch, fill a grid before prompting:

| Shot | Camera | Foreground | Leading line | Giant form | Cosmic anchor | Motion |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | low | gate/base | diagonal rise | threshold | sun | dolly + crane |
| 2 | eye | water/corridor | axial passage | bridge/city | none | lateral drift |
| 3 | high oblique | cliff/roof | spiral/terrace | relief/mountain | cloud horizon | descending reveal |
| 4 | aerial/top-down | open/weather | radial/river | planetary complex | Earth/moon | crane back/slow descent |

Change at least three columns between neighboring shots.

## Cultural checks

- Does the space remain identifiable after removing costumes?
- Are landscape, architecture, materials, and ritual mutually plausible?
- Is sacred writing absent or genuinely supplied by the user?
- Are living people treated as participants rather than exotic spectacle?
- Is one denomination or regional tradition chosen coherently?
- Does the cosmic anchor support rather than erase the culture?
