# Example: 冰岛 → 通用巨物世界

```text
Keyword: 冰岛
Type: country / region
Visual thesis: 冰岛 × 黑沙与冰川河 × 边缘生存 × 横跨极昼天空的玄武岩水门
Reality baseline to reject: 一座真实冰岛瀑布、火山或玄武岩教堂的旅游大片
Giant transmutation: 一只半沉在北大西洋中的大陆尺度玄武岩巨手，手掌成为群岛地貌
Category fusion: 人体 × 柱状玄武岩地质 × 地热水利城市
Impossible law: 掌纹控制冰川河与地热蒸汽的流向，水流越过指节后形成海上瀑布
Foreground family: wet black-sand shelf pressed against one weathered fingertip
Midground: a geothermal road and clustered turf settlements crossing the finger joint
Tiny figures: three travelers below 1% frame height inspecting a hot-water channel
Atmosphere: glacier mist passes between the stone fingers; distant ocean horizon remains visible
World-body: the basalt hand controls most of the frame, with inhabited palm lines and bridges cut through the knuckles
Three scale proofs: tiny travelers; villages and roads attached to the hand; ocean weather and waterfalls interacting with its anatomy
Cosmic anchor: one pale low sun
Camera: water-level 14 mm extreme proximity, diagonal road entering the palm, full hand silhouette still readable through haze
Absurdity score: 10/10
```

The final image prompt should preserve Icelandic geology, turf construction, geothermal infrastructure, contemporary clothing, and North Atlantic light while making the premise impossible. Avoid Viking stereotypes, fantasy castles, a normal volcano postcard, and a merely enlarged stone sculpture.
