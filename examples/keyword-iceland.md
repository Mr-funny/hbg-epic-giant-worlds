# Example: 冰岛 → 通用巨物世界

```text
Keyword: 冰岛
Type: country / region
Visual thesis: 冰岛 × 黑沙与冰川河 × 边缘生存 × 横跨极昼天空的玄武岩水门
Foreground family: reflective black-sand water, no tree
Midground: a narrow geothermal footpath with three tiny travelers
Atmosphere: glacier mist and distant volcanic rain
Megastructure: one basalt aqueduct-temple derived from columnar geology
Cosmic anchor: one pale low sun
Camera: water-level 16 mm, diagonal leading line, full structure visible
```

The final image prompt should preserve contemporary Icelandic plausibility, avoid Viking stereotypes, and use tiny people, water reflection, basalt scale, and atmospheric depth rather than fantasy-castle ornament.
